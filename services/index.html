Questions

What does an AI video creator do?

I make commercial video where generative AI does part of the work: scenes that would be expensive or impossible to film, characters, voices and music. I combine it with real camera footage and classic editing, so the result looks like one film, not a set of AI clips.

Do you only make AI video?

No. I have been filming and editing commercial work for about three years: fashion, sportswear, car detailing, furniture. AI is one of the tools; where a camera works better, I film it live.

Will AI change how my product looks?

Not when the work is careful. I work from your real product photos or footage, keep the geometry, materials and logo, and check every shot. If something is off, I fix it or replace it with a real shot.

Can you work for our agency under NDA and white label?

Yes. I can sign an NDA, deliver under your brand and keep the work private unless you agree to show it.

Do you make the sound and music too?

Yes. Sound design, voice-over (including AI voices in different languages) and music are part of the job, so the video comes finished. I also make my own music as Mild 9 Records.

Which formats do you deliver?

16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and other formats, for websites, ads, YouTube, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Where are you based? Do you work remotely?

I live in Warsaw, Poland. I shoot on location in Warsaw and work remotely with clients anywhere. I speak Ukrainian, Polish and English.

Can you build an interactive website like this one?

Yes. I can make a 3D site for a brand, a product launch or a portfolio, with your films inside, sound and a guide, readable by search engines.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length, how much is filmed or generated, and the deadline. Send a short brief and you will get a quote.

Is AI content labelled?

Yes. AI-generated scenes and AI voices are marked, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act.
